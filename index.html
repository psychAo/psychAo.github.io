Chao Song

Welcome to my personal webpage! I am currently a PhD student in the School of Electronics and Information at Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), where I also completed my Master's degree. Prior to this, I obtained my Bachelor of Science from Huaqiao University (HQU).
My research interests include remote sensing and deep learning, with a particular focus on intelligent interpretation tasks for remote sensing imagery under complex variations.
